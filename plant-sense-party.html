ABOUT THIS PROJECT

As living organisms, plants constantly perceive their environment with various information-collecting terminals. In the inaudible world of plants, they have their own way of sensing the world.

Plant Sense Party uses visualization to transform bioelectric signals into perceivable media. Through the five senses of plants, we attempt to converse with them, amplify their microscopic behaviors, and create a wonderful plant sensory world.
