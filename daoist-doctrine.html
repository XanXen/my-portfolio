ABOUT THIS PROJECT

Inspired by the Daoist philosophy of "Wu Wei" (non-action), this interactive installation explores the harmony between human intention and natural flow. Through gesture and presence, participants experience the paradox of effortless action.

The installation invites visitors to engage with generative visuals that respond to stillness and movement, revealing the beauty of letting go and allowing things to unfold naturally — a digital meditation on the Daoist doctrine of inaction.
